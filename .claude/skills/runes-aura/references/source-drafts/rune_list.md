# List of Runes

## 1. Crafted Runes

**Status:** No longer exist physically. Created by forge reactions.

- **Chaos** — the corruptive principle. Created by a Thalori using the rare material **komatiite**.
- **Order** — the harmonizing principle. Prototyped by a Thalori during the **Endless War** using a rare material; it was incomplete and too late to be tested further.

**Notes:** The Endless War ended everything, so an Order rune never fully emerged. **Lewis Wright** will aim to create one in **Book 2**. Consider whether a villain later discovers how to create more **Chaos** runes as well.

## 6. Animal Runes

**Found in all kingdoms.** Regional tendencies:

- **Sylvara** skews *Scale* + *Swarm*; also abundant *Beasts* + *Avian*.
- **Veridor** skews *Aquatic*; strong *Beasts* + *Avian* along plains/coasts.
- **Arkazia** skews *Beasts* + *Avian*; *Scale* moderate; *Aquatic/Swarm* localized (rivers, old‑growth groves).
- **Draxys** skews *Scale* + *Swarm*; *Avian* present; *Aquatic* rare.
- **Lumus** skews *Aquatic* + *Avian*; *Swarm* (crustaceans) moderate.
- **Nordalh** skews *Beasts* + *Avian* (+ coastal *Aquatic*); *Scale* scarce.
- **Zandres** skews *Scale* + *Swarm*; *Avian* common; *Aquatic* minimal.
- **Drakanith** skews *Avian* + *Beasts*; *Scale* present; *Aquatic* scarce.
- **Moraphys** skews *Scale* + *Swarm* + *Aquatic*; *Beasts/Avian* lower.

**Mythical creatures** are **Unique Runes** (one‑of‑a‑kind), appearing very rarely across all kingdoms.

### Categories

#### Beast (Mammals)

Warm‑blooded vertebrates with hair/fur; females produce milk.  
**Rune themes:** strength, endurance, grapples, pack tactics.  
**Examples:** Lion, Bear, Wolf, Elephant, Monkey.

#### Avian (Birds)

Warm‑blooded vertebrates with feathers, beaks, hard‑shelled eggs, wings.  
**Rune themes:** speed, agility, flight/glide, precision vision.  
**Examples:** Eagle, Penguin, Ostrich, Hummingbird, Peacock.

#### Scale (Reptiles + Amphibians)

Cold‑blooded vertebrates. Reptiles have scales; amphibians undergo metamorphosis and have permeable skin.  
**Rune themes:** defense/armor, stealth/camouflage, ambush, venom, regeneration, powerful leaps.  
**Examples:** Snake, Crocodile, Turtle, Chameleon, Frog.

#### Aquatic (Fish + Mollusks)

Fish are gill‑breathing, finned vertebrates; mollusks are soft‑bodied invertebrates (incl. cephalopods).  
**Rune themes:** aquatic mobility (dash/jet), sonar/lure, shocks, camouflage/ink, pressure/deep‑sea effects.  
**Examples:** Shark, Electric Eel, Anglerfish, Octopus, Squid.

#### Swarm (Insect + Arachnid + Crustacean + Myriapod)

All arthropods: insects, spiders/scorpions, crabs/shrimp, centipedes/millipedes.  
**Rune themes:** carapace armor, webs/traps, stings/venom, pincers/grapples, swarm tactics.  
**Examples:** Butterfly (Insect), Honeybee (Insect), Praying Mantis (Insect), Spider (Arachnid), Crab (Crustacean), Centipede (Myriapod).

#### Mythical

Unique Runes—each is singular and rare. Covers rare and legendary creatures.  
**Rune themes:** signature powers tied to the creature’s legend or ancient ecology.

**Examples:**  
*Beasts* — Cerberus, Minotaur, Chimera, Unicorn  
*Avian* — Phoenix, Pegasus, Griffin  
*Scale* — Dinosaur, Dragon, Basilisk  
*Aquatic* — Hydra, Kraken, Leviathan  
*Swarm* — Karkinos (Giant Crab), Ōmukade (Giant Centipede), Tsuchigumo (Giant Spider)

## 7. Elemental Runes

- **Fire** — *Thermal energy, combustion, radiant heat.*  
  **Themes:** ignition, jets, heat halos, glare.

- **Water** — *Fluids, pressure, cohesion.*  
  **Themes:** water‑cut, surge/flow control, mist veils.

- **Wind** — *Air pressure and motion.*  
  **Themes:** cutters, knockback, shockbursts.

- **Earth** — *Stone/soil density and stability.*  
  **Themes:** tremors, bulwarks, spikes.

### Fused Elementals

*Fusion occurs during runeforge; elemental runes have synergy.*

- **Steam** *(Fire + Water)* — *Scalding vapor and pressure differentials; fog that bites.*  
  **Themes:** scald veils, pressure jets/cones, concealment fogs, heat‑soak terrain.

- **Sand** *(Wind + Earth)* — *Airborne grit shaped into dunes and abrasive storms.*  
  **Themes:** sandblasts, blindness, erosion, shifting ground, partial burial/pinning.

- **Mud** *(Water + Earth)* — *Viscous earth‑water slurry that grips and drags.*  
  **Themes:** suction fields, slows/snares, bog walls, sludge armor, grapple pulls.

- **Lightning** *(Fire + Wind)* — *Ionized arcs and overpressure—speed made electric.*  
  **Themes:** chained bolts, stuns/disrupt, overcharge dashes, conductive marks.

- **Ice** *(Water + Wind)* — *Super‑cooled water shaped by airflow; hard frost geometry.*  
  **Themes:** frost lances, armor chill, slick slides, freeze/fragment control.

- **Lava** *(Fire + Earth)* — *Molten rock with crust‑over shells and heat haze.*  
  **Themes:** eruption pools, splash burn, crust plating, lingering scorch zones.
