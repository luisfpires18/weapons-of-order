# ☠️ Weapons of Chaos
# Forged through unspeakable rituals during the Endless War by the Thalori Race, the Weapons of Chaos are soul-bound weapons, each containing a trapped spirit.
They speak to their wielders, promising power but spreading corruption. Over time, they don’t just empower — they consume.
Each weapon is tied to a unique Corrupted Rune (a normal rune fused with a Chaos Rune) and adds a new layer/level in aura progression, the Dreadform.
These weapons played a pivotal role in the founding conflicts between early kingdoms.
Akron Wright, the greatest known blacksmith, came closer than anyone to unlocking their origin — but critical truths remain lost.

### Sanguessuga (Two Handed Sword split into multiple swords)
- Corrupted Rune: Blood (Life + Chaos)
- Domain: Hunger, parasitic growth
- Special Trait: Consumes blood to grow. Eventually splits into autonomous blade clones that attack independently.
- Description: A grotesque greatsword of pulsating crimson flesh and blackened bone. Its surface writhes with exposed veins that twitch when near life. It breathes faintly—hungry.

### Hailbane (Two-Handed Axe)
- Corrupted Rune: Hail (Frost + Chaos)
- Domain: Piercing frost storm/hailstorm
- Special Trait: Emits hail shards with each swing, freezing terrain and puncturing armor.
- Description: Forged from fractured obsidian ice, glowing cyan runes pulse across its surface. When swung, hail rises unnaturally from the ground, impaling foes in frigid arcs.

### Mournebreaker (Two Handed Hammer)
- Corrupted Rune: Bone (Decay + Chaos)
- Domain: Skeletal armor eruption
- Special Trait: Embeds bone fragments into terrain. Repeated strikes awaken skeletal limbs beneath enemies.
- Description: A colossal hammer made of fused vertebrae and fractured skulls. It groans as it's lifted. Striking with it makes the earth bleed bones.

### Pestilence (Bow)
- Corrupted Rune: Plague (Poison + Chaos)
- Domain: Infection, sickness
- Special Trait: Arrows infect targets on contact, spreading plague through proximity.
- Description: Bow of rotting wood, bound by dark sinew. Arrows hiss and steam, dripping contagion with every pull.

### Calamur (Gauntlets)
- Corrupted Rune: Destruction (Force + Chaos)
- Domain: Explosive aura shock
- Special Trait: Each punch triggers a concussive energy burst. Chains together to destabilize enemy aura fields.
- Description: Gauntlets of chained obsidian plates, cracked with glowing red lightning runes. Each strike echoes like thunder—heavy and volatile.

### Ashbrand (Spear)
- Corrupted Rune: Magma (Lava + Chaos)
- Domain: Lava ignition
- Special Trait: Dual-purpose spear. Can be thrown or wielded in close quarters. Impacts trigger lava chain eruptions.
- Description: Shaft of cracked obsidian, core of molten ember. Each throw leaves scorched trails and boiling craters.

### Victura (Scythe)
- Corrupted Rune: Necrosis (Spirit + Chaos)
- Domain: Soul decay, undeath
- Special Trait: Harvests the souls of the dead to fuel bone-wrapped rotlings that briefly serve in battle.
- Description: Shaft of shifting bone and death-wrought metal. The blade curves beyond reason. It drinks from the fallen.

### Wanegrip (Staff)
- Corrupted Rune: Psychic (Mind + Chaos)
- Domain: Mental fracture
- Special Trait: Distorts perception. Targets hear false orders, see illusions, and forget intent mid-action.
- Description: A staff formed of fossilized brain matter wrapped around a spiraling black crystal. At its peak floats an eye-shaped gem that never blinks.

### Vantashields (Dual Wield Shields)
- Corrupted Rune: Metorite (Obsidian + Chaos)
- Domain: Impact shock, ricochet shrapnel, low-grav crater wakes
- Special Trait: Starfall Riposte — A perfect block stores impact spall in both rims; releasing a bash hurls one shield on a curving meteor path that returns to hand and detonates the stored -spall into a short, forward shrapnel fan.
- Description: Twin bucklers faced with pitted meteor glass and iron-nickel studs. Parries glaze the rims in a brief star-sheen; steps trail ash crescents. Holding guard fuses their profiles into a momentary wide pavise as molten flecks slough off to screen incoming fire.

### Thricefang (Claw Gauntlets)
- Corrupted Rune: Monstrosity (Mythical + Chaos)
- Domain: Cerberus rage, multi-headed assault
- Special Trait: Triple-hit combos. Each successive hit devours aura, fractures will, and summons fear.
- Description: Gauntlets shaped like gnashing Cerberus maws. The third strike always hits harder than it should.

### Ruincoils (Whips)
- Corrupted Rune: Entropy (Circuit + Chaos)
- Domain: Conduction, circuit collapse, overload feedback
- Special Trait: Feedback Loop — first lash tags the target with a volatile circuit; second routes current through armor, wet stone, and chains to double reach; third collapses the loop, shredding shields, unspooling aura constructs, and stunning the body.
- Description: Paired cable-whips with braided conductor cores that extrude blue-white ion filaments. Each crack seeds “decay nodes” across metal and mechanisms; loops can flare into brief entropy halos to shear projectiles or yank foes off balance.

### Boreal Halos (Dual Wield Chakram)
- Corrupted Rune: Aurora (Light + Chaos)
- Domain: Prismatic flare, spectral afterimages, disorientation veils
- Special Trait: Whiteout Crossing — Thrown rings leave lingering light-arcs; when two arcs intersect, they trigger a whiteout burst that blinds, strips minor illusions, and tugs targets a step toward the crossing.
- Description: Paired ring-blades with refractive, mirror-bright edges. Each release paints an auroral band that the returning halo rides; tight doubles cut clean lanes through mooks, while staggered throws weave a moving maze of glare and ghost-images.
## 🌟 WEAPONS OF ORDER – This is a work in progress only.
Forged through the infusion of Order with natural runes, these weapons embody resonance, clarity, and ascension. They contain no soul, only harmony—and grow through discipline.

### Confirmed:

### Twinblades (Dual Wield Double-Edged Swords)
- Purified Rune: Eclipse (Dark + Order)
- Domain: Ordered umbra, captured force, timed counters
- Special Trait: Corona Rebound — perfect parries store force in concentric rings; release to unleash escalated counter-cuts that get brighter/heavier each rebound.
- Description: Matched moon-dark blades with pale temper lines. Their shadows tighten into neat halos that snap outward on command.

### Bastionreach (Tower Shield)
- Purified Rune: Aegis (Earth + Order)
- Domain: Unbreakable defense
- Special Trait: Reflecting Ward — converts absorbed aura into a line-true return blast; can anchor to terrain to ignore knockback.
- Description: Granite faced, gold inlay ribs. Each impact sets tidy fault rings that roll back on your mark.

### Harmony (Dual Wield Crossbows)
- Purified Rune: Energy (Lightning + Order)
- Domain: Precision resonance, radiant disruption
- Special Trait: Metered Volley — alternate-hand firing keeps a constant beat; bolts shatter corrupted aura and briefly reveal hidden paths.
- Description: Slim crystal-limb crossbows that chime softly as the strings self-tune; shots leave straight, needle-thin tracer lines.

### Everbloom (Quarterstaff)
- Purified Rune: Wood (Life + Order)
- Domain: Growth, binding, gentle renewal
- Special Trait: Living Form — the staff extends/retracts to form vine-loops, lattice-bridges, or rooting spikes; perfect blocks sprout healing buds.
- Description: Polished heartwood with vein-green sigils. Vines unspool on swings and re-weave into smooth grain between strikes.

### Prismglaive (Glaive)
- Purified Rune: Quartz (Crystal + Order)
- Domain: Refraction, clarity, illusion break
- Special Trait: Prism Guard — swings lay refraction lanes; projectiles entering are redirected to safe vectors or split into harmless glints.
- Description: Clear-edged blade with facet-veins. On perfect angle, the edge emits a tight spectral rim that trims phantoms to nothing.

### Bulwarkers (Pummelers)
- Purified Rune: Protect (Fortitude + Order)
- Domain: Pure mitigation, counter discipline
- Special Trait: Sanctum Knuckles — guarding projects a short bastion that reduces damage and grants guard-break immunity; perfect blocks auto-trigger a stagger cone.
- Description: Heavy bracers and fist cores plated in calm silver. Each beat-down feels measured—impact without chaos.

###  Skyrend Talons (Claw Gauntlets)
- Purified Rune: Ascendant (Beast + Order) — variant: Horse → Pegasus
- Domain: Grace, aerial control, righteous ferocity
- Special Trait: Triple Step — gain triple dash, mid-air counters, and short glide; finishers leave ascending wind-marks allies can follow.
- Description: Sleek claw gauntlets with feathered filigree and a braced vambrace. Jumps feel guided—as if the air knows your next move.
