# - RUNEFORGED WEAPONS
Runeforged weapons are made and can be either L0, L1, L2 by unlocking (very hard to unlock levels, specially L2). A L0 is a normal weapon with runes inscribed, called dormant weapon. A L1 weapon is called Enhanced while L2 is called Artifact. They can’t access L3 without a crafted rune.
Weapons of Order and Chaos are runeforged weapons crafted by the fusion of the Crafted Runes made by Universe Reactions (Chaos and Order). You can’t runeforge a weapon with just the chaos/order rune unlike the orders (rule 1 rune per weapon) – but the chaos / order allows the merge with another rune, enhancing it. A example is if you combine a mind (mystic rune) with chaos you get a Psychic Rune. A corrupted rune. When runeforging a fusion like that you get the weapons of chaos. And the chaos component allows a L3
- One rune per weapon. The rune defines the weapon’s full identity, look, and behaviour.
- Runes are found, not crafted — they exist naturally in the world as Runestones.
- The only runes crafted — Chaos and Order – reactions/experiments made. They do not function alone.

## - Blacksmith Techniques
Very hard to achieve, only a few can do it.
Rune extraction: Able to extract the rune from the weapon into a stone, so you can use it for another weapon later.
Blood infusion: Infuses blood of the wielder into the weapon. Benefit: It bounds the user to the weapon, and the weapon will only allow to be wielded by him. Learned by Akron Wright first.
Soul Infusion: Infused part of the soul of the wielder into the weapon. Benefit: Weapon is slightly stronger. Probably has a higher chance of achieving L2. Can’t be mixed with Blood Infusion. Downsize: If either the weapon or the wielder dies, the other part also dies. Learned by Akron Wright first.
Soul Entrapment: A requirement for the creation of weapons of chaos / order. The crafted runes demands a living host, not a part of the soul inside the weapon. Forbidden technique only mastered by the Thalori when creating the weapons of chaos. Unknown to humans. Learned by Lewis Wright first.

### Weapons to be used on lore
### All weapons:

- Edged Weapons: Sword, Dagger, Axe, Two-Edged Sword;
- Blunt Weapons: Mace, Hammer, Flail, Club;
- Pole Weapons: Polearm, Spear, Glaive, Lance, Pike, Scythe;
- Defensive Weapons: Shield (Round, Kite, Tower), Trident & Net;
- Ranged Weapons: Bow, Crossbow, Javelin, Throwing-Shield, Chakram, Shuriken;
- Fist Weapons: Claw Gauntlets, Pummelers;
- Staff Weapons: Staff, Scepters, Rods, Quarterstaff;
- Flexible Weapons: Whips, Three-Section Staff;


### Conditioned weapons by rune type:
Mystic Runes will auto-reshape the weapon into a Staff Weapon except Quarterstaff. Quarterstaff is for melee combat.
Animal Runes will auto-reshape the weapon into a Claw Gauntlets (≈28–40 cm) + Spiked vambrace;
Physical Runes will auto-reshape the weapon into a Pummeler or Three-Section Staff Weapon.
Material Runes will auto-reshape the weapon into a weapon not belonging to the categories: Fist, Staff and Flexible.

### Weapon wield type:
Always 2 SLOTS (one hand each)
Two Handed Weapon (2 slots)
Dual-Wielding Weapons (2 slots, 1 slot each) – mirror weapons
One Handed Weapon + Off Hand Weapon (2 slots, 1 slot each)

- Notes:
Bows always 2-handed (2 slots);
Trident and Net must be a combination of 2 slots.
