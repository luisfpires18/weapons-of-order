- **AURA LEVELS**

# LEVEL 0 – DORMANT
The weapon carries no active power, but the rune’s symbols are etched into its form, faintly visible on the surface.

# LEVEL 1 – CONDUIT
The weapon channels the aura of its rune, allowing the wielder to unleash attacks—defensive or offensive—through it. When drawn for battle, the weapon glows with that aura’s energy.

## **Elemental Runes**

- **Fire:** Steel glows red-hot; edges drip sparks or tongues of flame. Slashes ignite trails of fire.
- **Water:** Blade ripples like liquid; droplets spill from swings. Cuts release arcs of pressured spray.
- **Wind:** Edges blur; faint whistling surrounds each strike. Movements whip gusts of air.
- **Earth:** Metal hardens and darkens; stone veining crawls across. Every blow lands with quake-like force.
- **Lightning:** Crackling arcs race along the weapon; each strike lashes out with a bolt.
- **Ice:** Surface frosts over; shards splinter off with each swing.
- **Steam:** Surface sweats with scalding vapor; swings vent pressurized cones that fog and burn.
- **Sand:** Edge grinds with airborne grit; cuts trail abrasive gusts that blind and erode.
- **Mud:** Metal slicks into clay-sheen; impacts splatter suctioning sludge that slows targets.
- **Lava:** Veins of molten glow run the blade; strikes splash embering droplets that scorch on contact.

## **Nature Runes**

- **Life:** Weapon sprouts living wood, vines, or flowers. Hits pulse with faint green healing or growth.
- **Decay:** Rust and fungus creep across steel. Blows shed necrotic haze that weakens whatever it touches.
- **Poison:** Surface glistens violet; mist or dripping toxins coat each strike.
- **Spirit:** Ethereal lines or runes overlay the steel. Can unleash Ghostly animal spirits from weapon as attack but they disappear on impact.

## **Animal Runes**

- **Beast:** Grip thickens with fur-like texture; claws or fangs edge the blade. Swings echo a roar.
- **Avian:** Feathers etch along the surface; strikes whistle sharp as a hawk’s dive.
- **Scale****:** Weapon glints like scale-plate; edges curve with reptilian fangs. Each cut carries a venomous hiss.
- **Aquatic:** Blade takes a sleek, fin-like edge; water droplets bead and spray. Swings slice like waves.
- **Swarm:** Chitin sheen covers it; edges split into claw-like mandibles. Impacts scatter sparks like insect wings.
- **Mythical:** Adopts traits of the chosen legend (phoenix feathers burning, dragon’s scale glow, Cerberus triple-gnash patterns).

## **Physical Runes**

- **Force:** The pummeler pulses with shockwaves; each swing hums like compressed air about to burst.
- **Fury:** The pummeler vibrates with momentum; combo strikes build in speed and power.
- **Fortitude:** Pummeler grows heavy and immovable, glowing with steady pressure. Blocks and counters are unyielding.
- **Flow:** Movements shimmer; parries redirect enemy force back in ripples.

## **Material Runes**

**The aura only alters the weapon’s metal (blade/head/guard). The hilt/handle stays normal and the wielder gains no body plating, magnets, or other side effects.**

- **Bronze:** A burnished umber skin plates the steel; the alloy spreads shock into the spine—parries deaden recoil and return a steady shove; a faint verdigris trace may bloom along the fuller.
- **Iron:** Localized magnetism lives on the edge/guard; bites deep and can tug target metal on contact or near-contact—never the wielder.
- **Obsidian:** A glossy black glaze coats the steel; on impact the edge micro-fractures, shedding outward razor splinters that fade after a beat.
- **Crystal:** Faceted prisms form along the fuller/edge; swings refract into tight light-rays from the steel itself.
- **Silver:** Polished argent sheen on the metal; cuts leave clean silver trails and purge minor taint where the blade touches.
- **Gold:** A warm resonance hum runs through the weapon’s body; strikes transmit heat/conductivity through the steel (handle insulated).
- **Titanium:** A matte gunmetal skin lightens the blade; it springs rather than bends—quick strings bank micro-flex that snaps into the final hit, adding piercing drive without sticking.
- **Platinum:** A cold, mirror-pale sheen wraps the metal; on contact the edge slips through grime and weak wards—cuts land “sterile,” and the heavier balance rewards calm, decisive swings.
- **Gemstone:** Fine, colored facet-grain freckles the edge; the micro-facets bite at single points—first pass scores, follow-ups track the facets and open clean with low drag. Emerald:green, Sapphire:blue, Ruby:red;
- **Diamond:** A clear lattice hardens the edge; impacts refuse to deform and break false/illusory constructs on the blade’s pass—unyielding.

## **Mystic Runes**

- **Mind:** Weapon shimmers, reflections doubling and twisting. Strikes carry psychic pressure or whispers.
- **Time:** Blades blur in after-images; impacts seem to echo a half-second apart.
- **Space:** Edges ripple with folded light; cuts distort distance, extending farther than they should.
- **Sound:** Weapon hums; swings release sharp sonic cracks.
- **Gravity:** Strikes drag downward, pulling foes or pinning blows heavier than steel should.
- **Sigil****:** Symbols flare across the surface; each strike leaves glowing glyphs that linger briefly.
- **Barrier:** Weapon projects translucent walls or planes when raised to block.
- **Shadow:** Edges darken into silhouette; swings trail with shadow clones or decoys.
- **Pulse****:** Weapon throbs in steady beats; each swing emits an expanding pulse-ring that staggers on contact and briefly amplifies allied actions taken on-beat.

## **Technic Runes**

- **Particle:** Blade edge flickers with annihilation sparks; cuts leave tiny bursts of white decay.
- **Nanite:** Surface crawls with shifting metal grains, reshaping subtly between swings.
- **Magnet:** Weapon pulls or repels nearby steel; edges hum with polarity arcs.
- **Capacitor:** Strikes store static charge, discharging with the next blow.
- **Circuit:** Faint gridlines glow; each cut leaves a residual crackle of blue energy.
- **Vector:** Trajectory lines shimmer; thrown weapons curve unnaturally back on course.

## **Primal Runes**

- **Light:** Radiant white aura sheaths the weapon; swings blind with searing arcs.
- **Dark:** Black fire bleeds from edges; strikes absorb light and echo with void.

# **LEVEL 2 — ASPECT FORM (Enhanced/Artifact Weapons)**
The wielder undergoes a staged transformation, gradually embodying the rune’s power. Think of this “Aspect Form” like an RPG class—each rune shapes the body and weapon in its own way.

## **Elemental****:**

The user manifests elemental traits, able to reshape body and weapon into the chosen element. Fused elements carry extra potency.

##

- **25% Minor Aspect** — A single limb (arm or leg) shifts into elemental form.
- **50% Greater Aspect** — Multiple limbs (arms and legs) transform together.
- **75% Major Aspect** — The whole body, except the head, shifts. The head shows subtle changes (cheeks, eyes, or hair).
- **100% Full Aspect** — Full embodiment. The entire body becomes a living elemental state.

**Water:** Fluid musculature; weapon hardens into a pressured water-blade. Cuts act like high-pressure arcs; stance flows around hits, then whips back with tidal force.
**Wind:** Weight drops; weapon hums with slipstream. Strikes shed gusts and vacuum slices; dashes become air-steps. Deflects curve projectiles off-line.

**Ice:** Frost lattice over skin; weapon grows a razor-ice geometry. Strikes leave rime; perfect impacts freeze edges of the field or shatter armor seams.
**Steam:** Scalding vapor skin; weapon steams and hisses. Pressure-cone blasts obscure sightlines and burn; vents let you slip through grapples.
**Sand:** Grit cloak; weapon grinds with abrasive flow. Cuts erode guards; gusts blind; ground shifts into dunes you can surf or bog enemies in.

# **LEVEL 3 — DREADFORM / ASCENDANT**

**Giant Aspect.** L3 is a giant-grade manifestation of the rune—an upgrade over L2 that magnifies body, reach, and field effects. Only weapons **fused with Order or Chaos** can reach this tier, and only if the weapon was forged with **soul-entrapment**. Enhanced/Artifact (non-Order/Chaos) weapons cannot attain L3.

## **Branches**

# **DREADFORM — Chaos**

A colossal, corrupted Aspect animated by the **tortured spirit bound in the weapon**. Its presence spreads taint, warps the battlefield, and pushes the wielder toward loss of control—the weapon’s voice gets louder than yours. Think hunger, overgrowth, entropy.

**Field tells**

- **Corruption zone:** Armor pits, flora blackens, aura constructs unravel near contact points.

- **Runaway growth:** Extra limbs/maws, bone or metal spurs, or parasitic clones; the form may evolve mid-fight.

- **Possession risk:** Prolonged use lets the spirit steer actions;

# **ASCENDANT — Order**

A colossal, purified Aspect shaped by **harmonic resonance**—sacrificed soul, with disciplined alignment. Terrain stabilizes; constructs become precise; allies feel steadier inside its light. Think clarity, geometry, sanctuary.

**Field tells**

- **Sanctum zone:** Lines of force “square” the field; projectiles curve into safe lanes; minor cleansing pulses steady allies.

- **Construct mastery:** Hard-light wings, prisms, or gravity rings form and reposition on intent.

- **Measured surge:** Power scales smoothly with control—flashier if the wielder keeps rhythm rather than rage.

## **Shared properties (both forms)**

- **Scale & time:** Typically 3–6× the wielder; sustained only in brief windows before collapse.

- **Apex move:** Each form expresses a single, scene-defining effect (e.g., blight wave vs. daybreak dome).

- **Aftermath:** Terrain keeps scars—blight fields or sanctum “prints” can linger long after; Moraphys bears the worst historical example of multiple Dreadforms colliding.
