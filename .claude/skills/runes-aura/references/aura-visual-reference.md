# Weapons of Order: Aura Visual Reference

This reference preserves useful visual/power direction from the creator's `aura_levels.md` draft while applying the current Rune/Aura canon.

It is a **visual and thematic reference**, not a locked combat-number specification.

Read `rune-canon.md` first when structural rules matter.

## Structural interpretation

Current Aura ladder:

`L0 Dormant -> L1 Conduit -> L2 Aspect -> L3 Dreadform / Ascendant`

Current meaning:

- **L0 Dormant:** Rune is bound but active Aura is not yet manifested.
- **L1 Conduit:** the wielder manifests the **weapon's Aura**. Power is primarily expressed through the weapon/set.
- **L2 Aspect:** the weapon's Aura begins interacting with the **wielder's own Aura**, producing body/form manifestation.
- **L3:** Chaos -> Dreadform; Order -> Ascendant. Exact gameplay/soul consequences remain deferred.

Aura mastery belongs to the **wielder per Rune identity**. Weapon category sets the manifestation ceiling.

The source draft's 25/50/75/100 Aspect stages are useful transformation language, but are not currently locked as exact gameplay progression thresholds or UI.

## Conditioned forms apply from L0

Do not reinterpret old L1/L2 prose as if every Rune remained on the original mundane weapon shape.

Canonical L0 conditioned forms:

- Mystic -> Staff Weapon, excluding Quarterstaff
- Animal -> Claw Gauntlets + spiked vambrace
- Physical -> Pummeler or Three-Section Staff
- Material -> weapon outside Fist, Staff and Flexible families

L1/L2 visuals must continue from that transformed identity.

## L1 Conduit: weapon-Aura expression

### Elemental

Weapon/set visibly channels the chosen element.

Themes from the draft:

- Fire: heated metal, flame/spark trails, ignition.
- Water: fluid/rippling surface, pressured arcs and spray.
- Wind: blurred edges, air cuts and gusts.
- Earth: hardened/stone-veined weapon, heavy quake-like impacts.
- Lightning: visible arcs, chained discharge.
- Ice: frost geometry and splintering shards.
- Steam: scalding vapor and pressure cones.
- Sand: abrasive grit, blindness/erosion themes.
- Mud: suctioning sludge and slowing/gripping themes.
- Lava: molten veins, ember splash and scorch.

### Nature

- Life: living growth, wood/vines/flowers, restoration theme.
- Decay: rust/rot/fungus, weakening and erosion theme.
- Poison: venom/toxic sheen/miasma.
- Spirit: spectral glyphs and spirit projections/companions.

### Animal

Animal Runeforging already produced Claw Gauntlets at L0.

At L1, apply creature traits/power through the canonical Claw Gauntlets and weapon Aura rather than reverting to generic blade language.

Broad category themes:

- Beast: strength, endurance, grapples, pack pressure.
- Avian: agility, precision, glide/flight motifs.
- Scale: armor, ambush, venom/camouflage/regeneration themes.
- Aquatic: pressure, water mobility, sonar/ink/shock themes.
- Swarm: carapace, webs/traps, stings/pincers/multi-hit themes.
- Mythical: unique creature-specific expression.

### Physical

Physical Runeforging already produced a Pummeler or Three-Section Staff at L0.

Themes:

- Force: compressed impact/shockwaves.
- Fury: momentum and escalating aggression.
- Fortitude: endurance, immovable guard/counters.
- Flow: timing, redirection and reflected force.

Draft Aura colors explicitly associated with Physical Runes:

- Force: Yellow/Gold
- Fury: Red/Crimson
- Fortitude: Blue/Cobalt
- Flow: White/Silver

These are Rune/Aura colors, not kingdom palettes.

### Material

Material L1 is intentionally weapon-focused.

The Aura alters/apply effects to the weapon's applicable material portions. The wielder does **not** gain body plating or living armor at L1.

Examples/themes from the draft:

- Iron: localized magnetic interaction through weapon contact/near-contact.
- Bronze: shock-dampening/steady guard alloy expression.
- Obsidian: brittle razor splinter/shard expression.
- Crystal: faceting/refraction/focus.
- Silver: clean/purifying contact theme.
- Gold: resonance/conductivity.
- Titanium: light/spring-hard recovery.
- Platinum: stability/control.
- Gemstone: precision facets.
- Diamond: unyielding lattice/truth-cut theme.

Exact combat mechanics remain to be authored/configured.

### Mystic

Mystic Runeforging already produced a Staff at L0.

L1 should express wizard-like power through the Staff.

Themes:

- Mind: telepathy/illusion/compulsion.
- Time: dilation, slow/haste, echo/rewind motifs.
- Space: portals/folds/blink/distance manipulation.
- Sound: vibration, shockwaves, silence/sonar.
- Gravity: pull/push, mass, float/pin.
- Sigil: wards, seals, traps, delayed glyphs.
- Barrier: force constructs, walls, shields, lanes.
- Shadow: cast-shadow/umbra manipulation, distinct from Primal Dark.
- Pulse: rhythm/cadence, synchronized pulses and disruption.

### Technic

Technic remains late-game/lore content.

Themes:

- Particle: beam/decay/scatter.
- Nanite: repair/reconfiguration.
- Magnet: polarity/pull/repel.
- Capacitor: charge storage/discharge.
- Circuit: network/link/EMP disruption.
- Vector: trajectory/velocity/ricochet.

Do not introduce Technic early merely because this reference exists.

### Primal

- Light: radiant/searing weapon Aura.
- Dark: void/black-fire/absence-of-light expression.

Light and Dark are singular Primal Runes.

## L2 Aspect: wielder-Aura interaction

At L2, body/form manifestation becomes the defining difference.

The weapon/set and wielder should read as one Aura expression rather than a stronger glow pasted onto L1.

### Elemental Aspect

The wielder increasingly embodies the chosen element.

Draft fantasy examples include:

- Fire: heat mantle / flame body expression.
- Water: fluid musculature / pressured-water form.
- Wind: lightened body / air-step expression.
- Earth: stone plating / increased mass and anchoring.
- Lightning: visible arcs through the body / burst movement.
- Ice: frost lattice / razor-ice geometry.
- Steam: vapor skin / pressure vents.
- Sand: grit cloak / shifting-ground expression.
- Mud: clay/sludge embodiment / pull and pin.
- Lava: molten core under crusted plates.

### Nature Aspect

Current source themes:

- Life -> Druid / living-grove expression.
- Decay -> Undead / revenant expression.
- Poison -> venom/chitin/miasma embodiment.
- Spirit -> Shaman / spirit-overlay and companion expression.

Final class names remain creator-authored unless explicitly locked elsewhere.

### Physical Aspect

Physical Aspect is primarily a body-Aura martial embodiment.

Pummeler/weapon form may fuse away into rune marks/body expression as the wielder becomes the weapon.

Draft themes include shock-palms, seismic throws, pressure rings, body runes and martial Aura manifestation.

### Animal Aspect

Animal Aspect is Shapeshifter-style embodiment.

The wielder increasingly adopts the chosen animal's anatomy, locomotion, senses and natural weapon behavior.

Claw Gauntlets can fuse into the forearms/body expression as Aspect develops.

Mythical transformations remain unique creature-specific forms.

### Mystic Aspect

Mystic Aspect is wizard-style body casting.

The Staff can hover/orbit under mental control while the wielder's body becomes an active casting focus.

Source motifs include body runes, altered eyes, robe/hood/hat language and increasingly independent Staff behavior.

Do not treat these costume details as mandatory equipment rules unless separately approved.

### Material Aspect

Material Aspect is where the living material armor/body-armament fantasy belongs.

The weapon may merge into the manifested armament while the wielder becomes plated/enveloped by the Material Rune's living expression.

This is deliberately not moved down into L1 and is a major reason ordinary armor is not Runeforged.

### Technic / Primal Aspect

Detailed source concepts may be used later as inspiration, but exact gameplay implementation is not currently required for v1.

## L3 boundary

Named Dreadform/Ascendant concepts exist in older source material and high-tier weapon references.

For current game implementation:

- do not infer exact L3 mechanics from descriptive prose;
- do not finalize soul costs/consequences;
- do not auto-complete missing Purified/Corrupted Rune combinations;
- treat Dreadform/Ascendant visuals as late-game reference material until the creator explicitly reopens L3 design.

## Implementation policy

When prototype Rune effects are needed before final authored effects exist:

- use simple configurable mechanics consistent with the Rune's documented theme;
- avoid inventing new canonical lore/effect names;
- keep coefficients/data separate from this reference;
- replace temporary effects freely after playtesting.
