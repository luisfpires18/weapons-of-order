## Animal Rune categories

Animal Rune categories:
- Beast
- Avian
- Scale
- Aquatic
- Swarm
- Mythical

### Beast
Themes: strength, endurance, grapples, pack tactics.
Draft examples: Lion, Bear, Wolf, Elephant, Monkey.

### Avian
Themes: speed, agility, flight/glide, precision vision.
Draft examples: Eagle, Penguin, Ostrich, Hummingbird, Peacock.

### Scale
Reptiles + amphibians.
Themes: defense/armor, stealth/camouflage, ambush, venom, regeneration, leaps.
Draft examples: Snake, Crocodile, Turtle, Chameleon, Frog.

### Aquatic
Fish + mollusks.
Themes: aquatic mobility, sonar/lure, shocks, camouflage/ink, pressure/deep-sea effects.
Draft examples: Shark, Electric Eel, Anglerfish, Octopus, Squid.

### Swarm
Insects + arachnids + crustaceans + myriapods.
Themes: carapace armor, webs/traps, stings/venom, pincers/grapples, swarm tactics.
Draft examples: Butterfly, Honeybee, Praying Mantis, Spider, Crab, Centipede.

### Mythical
Unique/singular creature Runes.
Draft examples include Cerberus, Minotaur, Chimera, Unicorn, Phoenix, Pegasus, Griffin, Dinosaur, Dragon, Basilisk, Hydra, Kraken, Leviathan, Karkinos, Omukade, Tsuchigumo.

Examples do not automatically mean every example is already encountered/available in the game.
